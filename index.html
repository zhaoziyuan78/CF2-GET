CF2-GET

Conditional Flow for Counterfactual Symbolic Music Genre Transfer

Ziyuan ZhaoRuiji YuDylan Xinming HouGus Xia

Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence, Abu Dhabi

Code on GitHub Paper Listen BibTeX

How would this passage sound had it been written in another genre? CF2-GET answers that counterfactual directly. It runs a conditional flow backward to recover the passage’s noise under its own genre, swaps the genre label, and runs the same flow forward. The noise stays fixed, so melody and harmony carry over while the style changes.

Abstract

Symbolic music genre transfer, as a subproblem of music style transfer, means to preserve the identifiable features such as melody contour and harmony structure while altering other genre-related features in a meaningful way. However, previous methods mostly rely on either paired data of transfer or an external auxiliary classifier. Causal counterfactual offers a new perspective where such transfer can be viewed as a 3-stage process: abduction, intervention and prediction. In this paper, we propose CF2-GET, a framework inspired by counterfactual inference that leverages a noise-shared conditional flow to do symbolic music genre transfer. We show that with simple causality-aware and MIDI-specific design, an optimal-transport conditional flow matching training on generative task can faithfully accomplish abduction of exogenous noise and cross-genre prediction. Experiments on a large-scale symbolic music dataset show that CF2-GET outperforms VAE and diffusion-based baselines on both genre transfer and content preservation, showing that a flow-based generative model can implicitly learn counterfactual inference of symbolic music.

Transfer and content together

CF2-GET has the highest transfer success and keeps pitch classes, melody contour and chroma far closer to the source than any baseline.

What the baselines lose

Latent MIDI-VAE moves furthest in embedding space, but its melody contour correlation falls to 0.009. DDIM Inversion loses temporal and harmonic continuity, and MIDI-VAE keeps local harmony but rarely reaches the target genre.

Why HSIC matters

Without the HSIC term the flow mostly reproduces the source. Content scores rise, but only 10.8% of outputs reach the target genre.
